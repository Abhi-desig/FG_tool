name: Numbers grid
description: Two or more figures, aligned as a grid and given room to read.
aspect: 4:5
when: Choose this whenever the copy carries TWO OR MORE separate figures - a subsidy and a rate, a capacity and a warranty, three prices.
tone: Medium. A short headline, then two to four short label-and-figure pairs. Each figure needs its own label and nothing else.
---
Layout: a flat deep field fills the frame. The upper 30% is held empty as a
reserved zone for the headline: one continuous field of that deep colour,
perfectly even and free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no texture, no
object, no rule. The centre holds a grid of figure blocks, evenly spaced on a
shared baseline grid with wide gutters. The lower 15% is a calm band for the
brand and contact line.
Scene: no photograph. A flat graphic field with one very subtle even gradient and
one narrow horizontal hairline separating the grid from the lower band.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 9%. Headline large and
extra-bold in the reserved zone. In the grid, each pair is a small
widely-tracked grey capitals label with its figure directly beneath it, set large
and extra-bold in the warm accent. Every figure sits on its own line, never
inside a sentence.
Shapes: no shapes anywhere in the poster except the single hairline rule.
Palette: four colours only - deep charcoal, off-white, one warm accent, one mid
grey.
