imperfection: a slightly uneven course, a worn step edge.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 9% from the left edge, inside
the reserved sky. Headline very large and extra-bold; any second line
medium-bold and clearly smaller directly beneath it.
Shapes: no shapes anywhere in the poster - no pills, circles, panels, bands or
rules.

grey.
