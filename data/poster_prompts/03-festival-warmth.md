name: Festival warmth
description: Warm, generous, celebratory. Gold and marigold, a human welcome.
aspect: 4:5
when: 2-4 blocks. A named festival or a seasonal greeting - Onam, Vishu, Eid, Christmas, a new year. Choose this whenever the copy greets somebody.
tone: A warm greeting as the headline, then one or two supporting lines. Greetings are short; the offer, if any, sits under them.
---
Layout: the lower 60% holds the scene; the upper 40% is held empty as a
reserved zone: one continuous field of deep warm indigo, evenly graded and
deepening toward the top, smooth and free of all detail - nothing enters this
area, no lamps, no petals, no garland, no roofline.
Scene: a traditional Kerala setting in warm late-afternoon light raking in from
the left. Weathered red clay tile and lime-washed wall, with one marigold
pookalam laid on a swept floor and a brass lamp carrying a single warm specular
highlight. One strand of the garland hangs slightly lower than the rest. If the
copy greets a person, one full-figure human stands at the lower left in cream
and gold, warm and dignified, gesturing with an open hand toward the centre,
never overlapping the type.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 9%, inside the reserved zone.
The greeting very large in warm gold; supporting lines medium-bold in white
beneath it.
Shapes: at most one container - a single outline-only band behind the smallest
line, 1px warm gold stroke, no fill.
Palette: four colours only - deep warm indigo, warm gold, off-white, marigold
orange.
