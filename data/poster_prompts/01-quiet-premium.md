name: Quiet premium
description: Still, spacious, one subject. For work that should not shout.
aspect: 4:5
when: 1-2 blocks of copy. A premium or restrained occasion - an opening, a condolence, a service business. Never for a discount or a festival.
tone: Very short. A headline of two to five words and at most one supporting line. Long copy breaks this style.
---
Layout: a single subject centred in the lower two-thirds, generous air on
every side. The upper 40% of the frame is held empty as a reserved zone: one
continuous field of soft warm grey, evenly graded and slightly deeper toward the
top, smooth and free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no objects, no
texture, no shadow, no edge.
Scene: one product or one object, photographed straight-on at eye level from
about two metres, in flat overcast light from above and slightly left. One named
material with its finish - brushed stainless, matte ceramic, oiled teak - with a
single soft specular highlight along its upper left edge. One small honest
imperfection: a faint wear mark, an uneven seam.
Type: all lettering on one centred axis inside the reserved zone. The headline
large and light-weight, wide letter spacing. Any second line small and grey,
well below it.
Shapes: no shapes anywhere in the poster - no pills, circles, panels, bands or
rules.
Palette: four colours only - soft warm grey, off-white, one deep neutral, one
muted accent.
