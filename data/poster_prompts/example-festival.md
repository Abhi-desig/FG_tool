A finished print poster for a shop in Kerala, portrait, ready to send to press.

Visual concept: {{concept}}

Set these words on the poster, spelled exactly as written and nothing else added:
Headline: {{main}}
Second line: {{h1}}
Third line: {{h2}}

Composition:
- The headline dominates, in the upper third, the largest thing on the poster.
- The second and third lines sit below it, clearly smaller, in reading order.
- Leave a calm margin on all four edges so nothing is lost when the poster is trimmed.
- One clear focal image, warm festive colour, high contrast between the words and
  whatever sits behind them.

Do not invent any word, number, price, date or phone number that is not written
above. Do not add a logo. Do not add a watermark or a border caption.
