name: Split panel
description: A hard vertical split: flat colour one side, photograph the other.
aspect: 4:5
when: 3-5 blocks. Good when the copy divides cleanly into a claim and a proof, or a before and an after.
tone: Medium-short. A headline and two or three lines, each a complete thought that can stand alone in a column.
---
Layout: the frame is split by one hard vertical edge at 45% from the left. The
left panel is a flat field of deep colour and holds every word. The right 55% is
one photograph, full-bleed to the top, right and bottom edges. The upper 25% of
the left panel is held empty as a reserved zone: one continuous field of that
flat colour, perfectly even and free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no
texture, no shape, no shadow.
Scene: in the right panel, the subject seen in a low three-quarter view from
about three metres, in directional side light raking from the right. One named
material with its finish carrying a single specular highlight, and one small
authentic imperfection.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 8% from the left edge, within
the left panel. Headline large and extra-bold; supporting lines medium and
clearly smaller, stacked beneath with generous leading.
Shapes: no shapes anywhere in the poster - the split edge is the only division.
Palette: four colours only - one deep saturated ground, off-white, one warm
accent, one mid grey.
