name: Offer block
description: One figure, enormous. Everything else gets out of its way.
aspect: 4:5
when: 2-4 blocks, and the copy's point is a price, a percentage or a saving. Choose this when one number is the whole message.
tone: A short hook, then the figure, then terms in small print. The figure must be a single short string - a price or a percentage.
---
Layout: a flat deep field fills the frame. The upper 30% is held empty as a
reserved zone: one continuous field of that same deep colour, perfectly even and
free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no texture, no object, no shadow.
The centre 45% belongs to one figure. The lower 25% is a calm band for terms.
Scene: no photograph. A flat graphic field only, with one subtle even gradient
deepening toward the bottom edge and one narrow band of slightly lighter tone
behind the figure.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 9%. A small widely-tracked
capitals hook in the reserved zone; then the figure, set enormous and extra-bold
in the alert colour, three to four times the height of anything else; then terms
in small grey type in the lower band, on their own line.
Shapes: exactly one shape - a single outline-only pill with a 1px stroke and
fully rounded ends, no fill, containing the shortest supporting line.
Palette: four colours only - deep navy, white, one alert accent, one mid grey.
