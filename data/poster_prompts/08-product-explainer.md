name: Product explainer
description: The product installed and in use, with the copy naming what it does.
aspect: 4:5
when: 3-5 blocks, and the copy explains or lists what something is or includes. Choose this for a specification, a package, an inclusion list.
tone: Medium. A headline naming the product, then two to four short feature lines. Features are noun phrases, not sentences.
---
Layout: the photograph occupies the lower 65% full-bleed to the left, right
and bottom edges. The upper 35% is held empty as a reserved zone: one continuous
field of pale cool grey, evenly graded and slightly deeper toward the top, smooth
and free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no object, no cable, no
shadow, no edge.
Scene: the product installed and in use, never floating and never on a plain
backdrop, in a recognisable specific setting. Seen straight-on at eye level from
about four metres in flat daylight from above and slightly left. One named
material with its finish - brushed stainless, powder-coated white steel - with
one clean specular highlight along its upper edge. One small authentic
imperfection in the setting: a scuffed skirting, an uneven tile course.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 9%. Headline large and bold in
the reserved zone; feature lines small, medium-weight, stacked directly beneath
it with tight even leading.
Shapes: no shapes anywhere in the poster - no pills, circles, panels, bands or
rules.
Palette: four colours only - pale cool grey, one deep neutral, one warm accent,
off-white.
