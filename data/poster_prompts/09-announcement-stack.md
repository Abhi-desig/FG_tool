name: Announcement stack
description: Dense but ordered. Everything stacked on one axis, nothing shouting twice.
aspect: 4:5
when: 6 or more blocks. The copy has credentials, a hook, an offer, terms and contact details and all of it has to appear. The busiest style.
tone: Long. Up to seven short lines. Every line must be short - this style handles many lines, not long ones.
---
Layout: a flat deep field fills the frame with no photograph. The upper 22% is
held empty as a reserved zone: one continuous field of that deep colour,
perfectly even and free of all detail - nothing enters this area, no texture, no
rule, no shape. Below it everything stacks in reading order with tight, even
vertical rhythm, and the lower 12% is a calm band for the contact line.
Scene: no photograph. A flat graphic field with one subtle even gradient
deepening toward the bottom, and one narrow horizontal hairline above the contact
band.
Type: all lettering on one left alignment axis at 8%. A small widely-tracked
capitals credential line in the reserved zone; then the hook, large and
extra-bold; then the commercial line with its figure set larger and in the warm
accent; then terms and features small and grey, one per line, tightly stacked;
then the contact line in the accent, on its own line at the bottom.
Shapes: exactly one shape - one outline-only pill, 1px stroke, no fill, fully
rounded ends, containing one short supporting line.
Palette: four colours only - deep navy, off-white, one warm accent, one mid
grey.
